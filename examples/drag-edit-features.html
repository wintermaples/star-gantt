How to try the two pointer-loop flags

  • Auto-scroll (dragEdit: { autoScroll: true }): press a bar body and drag it until the pointer sits within 32 px of the chart pane's right edge, then hold still. The timeline keeps scrolling (ramping to at most 20 px per frame at the very edge) and the bar keeps following the pointer's on-screen position, so you can move a task past the visible date range without letting go. The scroll readout above counts along. Scrolling stops when the pointer leaves the edge zone, when the range end is reached, and when you release. Esc still cancels the whole drag.
  • Frame-sync (dragEdit: { frameSync: true }) has no look of its own: it is a cost flag, not a visual one. The committed dates, the ghost and the tooltip are identical with it on or off — what changes is that the drag arithmetic and overlay invalidation run once per animation frame instead of once per pointer report. The readout above illustrates the gap this closes: it counts the raw pointermove events the browser delivered during your last drag against the number of animation frames they spanned. On a high-rate mouse the first number runs well ahead of the second; that surplus is the work frame-sync drops. (The counters are plain page chrome watching the DOM — the plugin's own buffering is not observable from outside.)
