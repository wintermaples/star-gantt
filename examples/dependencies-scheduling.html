StarGantt v2 — dependencies and scheduling

What's on this page
  • Move a task: drag a task bar to change its start time.
  • Dependencies: move an upstream task and its dependents slide with it.
  • Cycle rejection: attempting a circular dependency is refused and reported.
  • Reconciler: choose between auto-schedule propagation (moves the whole downstream closure) and snap's pushSuccessors (propagation off; snap pushes violated successors forward by exactly their deficit — snap stands down automatically whenever propagation is on, so exactly one of the two ever runs).
  • Undo / redo: toolbar buttons or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z.
  • Scheduling row acts on the live chart — no rebuild, no reload.
