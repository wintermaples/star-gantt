StarGantt v2 — drag editing and undo/redo

How to use
  • Move a bar: drag a bar to change the task's start and end dates.
  • Resize: drag the handles at either end of a bar to stretch or shrink its duration.
  • Adjust progress: drag the progress handle inside a bar to change the completion ratio.
  • Undo: use the toolbar button or Ctrl+Z to revert the last change.
  • Redo: use the toolbar button, Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y to reapply a reverted change.
  • Keyboard editing: every drag above has a keyboard equivalent. Focus a row in the grid (click it, or Tab in), then: Ctrl+← / Ctrl+→ move the task by one unit of the current zoom scale; Ctrl+Shift+← / Ctrl+Shift+→ resize its end; Ctrl+Shift+↑ / Ctrl+Shift+↓ step its progress by 10%.
  • Task-edge magnet: turn Magnet on in the toolbar to enable the snap plugin's opt-in alignToTasks rule. An edited date that lands within the chosen pixel tolerance of another task's start or end sticks to that instant exactly, skipping the usual day rounding — drag a bar next to Vendor review, whose dates sit deliberately off the day grid, and watch the readout report the match. Switching the magnet or its tolerance re-creates the chart (the chart and its history start over), because plugin options are chosen when the chart is created.
