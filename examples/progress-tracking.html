StarGantt v2 — progress tracking

What's on this page RAG badges (design=green, impl=amber, test=red), the status-date zigzag progress line (toggleable), the bulk-update panel (edit progress % / remaining work for every task in one undo step) and the progress-trend panel (a small polyline over the seeded snapshots plus one you can record live). The toolbar below opens the on-demand panels through the real `stargantt.progress` service — nothing here is a shortcut around the public API.
